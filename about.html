The Irvin Family

Built on decades of
hands-on expertise.

The Irvin family has been in the elevator business since the 1960s. Todd and his brother Tim spent summers in the 1980s working alongside their father — disassembling elevators in the garage, learning the trade the way it used to be taught.

Todd didn't stumble into this industry. He was born into it, built his skills in it, and in 2006 founded Executive Elevator Co. on the foundation his father laid. Today the company operates across four markets, employs approximately 30 people, and has installed and serviced elevators for some of the most demanding clients in the region.

The clients — Rolls-Royce, Brown-Forman, Amazon, the Speed Art Museum — didn't find Executive Elevator through an ad. They found them the same way serious clients always find the right firm: word of mouth, reputation, and results.
